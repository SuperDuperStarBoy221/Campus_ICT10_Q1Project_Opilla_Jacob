📚 How SKU Works

Format Structure

SKU = [Category (3)] - [Product Name (4)] - [Stock Qty]

Example: ELE-LAPT-100
ELE = Electronics
LAPT = Laptop
100 = Stock Quantity

Benefits

  • ✓ Easy product identification
  • ✓ Inventory tracking
  • ✓ Quick reordering
  • ✓ Prevents duplication
